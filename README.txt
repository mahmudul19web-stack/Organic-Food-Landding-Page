VERDANT HARVEST — ORGANIC FOOD LANDING PAGE

Included:
- index.html
- assets/style.css
- assets/script.js

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. An internet connection is needed for Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, Google Fonts, and remote Unsplash photos.

Features:
- Responsive Bootstrap 5 layout
- Green, yellow, and warm cream palette
- Animated hero section and scroll reveal
- Three product cards, favourites, and demo basket notifications
- Newsletter demo form
- Mobile navigation and footer

Notes:
- Product prices, brand copy, and contact details are sample content.
- The basket and newsletter are front-end demos only; connect a backend/payment service for real orders.
- Photos load from Unsplash URLs and require internet access.
